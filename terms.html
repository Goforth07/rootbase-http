Daily Snapshots. The App keeps up to seven daily snapshots of your plants and care history on your device. Snapshots do not include photos; a restore from a snapshot uses the photos still on your device. Snapshots are removed when you uninstall the App.

Restore. Restoring from a snapshot or a backup file replaces the plants, care history, and photos currently in the App with the contents of the snapshot or backup. This cannot be undone. If iCloud Sync is on, the restore also replaces the data on your other devices using iCloud Sync, and changes they have not yet synced are lost. Only restore backup files that were created by RootBase and that you trust. We are not responsible for data loss or other issues caused by restoring a file that is incomplete, modified, corrupted, or not created by RootBase.

iCloud Sync. iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on and requires an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. When it is on, the App keeps your plants, care history, and photos the same on every device signed in to that account. Please note:

  • Sync is not a backup. Changes and deletions made on one device, including a restore, are applied to your other devices.
  • If iCloud already holds RootBase data when you turn sync on, the App asks whether to use the data in iCloud, use this device's data, or merge both. Using one side's data replaces the other. Merging keeps both, so plants that exist on both sides will appear twice.
  • Photos sync over Wi-Fi only unless you allow cellular data in the App's settings.
  • Sync runs in the background and may be delayed or fail, for example due to network conditions, insufficient iCloud storage, or iCloud being unavailable. We do not guarantee that any change will be synced or current.
  • Turning sync off keeps the data on your device. Turning it off or uninstalling the App does not remove your data from iCloud; you can manage or delete it through your device's iCloud storage settings.
