Support

RootBase 2.0 is rebuilt from the ground up as a native iPhone app. It requires iOS 26 or later. Settings are behind the gear in the top corner of the Home screen.

Updating to 2.0

What happens to my plants when I update?

The first time RootBase 2.0 opens, it brings over your plants, care history, and photos from the previous version automatically. There is nothing to export or set up.

Can I restore a backup I made with an earlier version?

Yes. Backup files exported from earlier versions of RootBase restore the same way as 2.0 backups. See How do I restore? below.

Data, Sync & Backups

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Sync, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud account. We do not run servers and cannot see your data.

How do I sync with iCloud?

Open Settings and tap iCloud Sync, then turn on Sync with iCloud. You need to be signed in to iCloud. Plants and their history always sync; photos wait for Wi-Fi unless you turn on Sync Photos on Cellular.

If iCloud already has a RootBase collection when you turn sync on, RootBase asks what to keep:

  • Use iCloud’s Data replaces what’s on this iPhone with the collection in iCloud. A copy of this iPhone’s data is kept on the device first.
  • Use This iPhone’s Data replaces the collection in iCloud, and on your other devices, with this iPhone’s.
  • Merge Both keeps both collections. Plants that are in both will appear twice.

How do I back up my plants?

RootBase keeps a daily snapshot of your plants and care history on your iPhone and holds on to the last seven. For a copy you control, open Settings, tap Backup & Restore, then Export Backup. This saves your plants, care history, and photos to a single file you can keep in Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere else.

How do I restore?

Open Settings and tap Backup & Restore. Pick one of the daily snapshots (they hold your plants and care history; photos come from this iPhone), or tap Choose File… to restore an exported backup. A restore replaces all of your current plants, care history, and photos, so export first if you want to keep what you have now. With iCloud Sync on, the restore replaces your collection on your other devices too.

Can I recover deleted plants?

Restoring a daily snapshot or an exported backup from before the plant was deleted brings it back. The restore replaces your whole collection with the one in that snapshot or file, so anything you changed since then is lost.

Care & Reminders

How does the watering schedule work?

RootBase uses an adaptive algorithm that learns how quickly each plant dries out. The more you log, the more accurate it becomes. If a plant isn’t ready when it comes up, tap Not Yet on the plant, or swipe it on the Home screen, to push the check back a few days.

How do I get reminders?

Open Settings and turn on Daily Check-In (when plants are ready to check) or Overdue Alert (when any plant is overdue), and choose a time for each. Both are off until you turn them on, and iOS asks for permission the first time. If notifications are turned off for RootBase in the iPhone Settings app, turn them back on there.

Turn on Early Reminders in Settings to be reminded a day early when a plant’s soil dries faster than expected.

Is there a Home Screen widget?

Yes. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for RootBase. The widget shows today’s plant care at a glance, in a green style or one that matches your system theme.
