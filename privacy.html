Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: RootBase does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on our servers. Your data stays on your device, and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud account. We never have access to it.

Who We Are

RootBase is built and maintained by RootBase Labs LLC, a company registered in Arkansas. In this policy, "we," "our," and "us" refer to RootBase Labs LLC.

Data Collection

RootBase does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. All data you enter into the app, including plant names, care logs, photos, notes, and schedules, is stored locally on your device. The only time this data leaves your device is if you turn on iCloud Sync, as described below, or send it somewhere yourself: by exporting a backup file, sharing a photo, or copying plant data to share with another app.

We do not use analytics services, advertising frameworks, crash reporting tools, or any third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Data Storage

Your plant data is stored in a local database on your device. RootBase also keeps up to seven daily snapshots of your plants and care history on your device so you can restore an earlier state. This data is not accessible to us or to any third party. We do not operate servers that receive, process, or store your information.

iCloud Sync

RootBase gives you the option to sync your data through iCloud so it stays the same on every device signed in to your Apple Account. iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings. When it is on, your plant data, including photos, care logs, notes, and schedules, is stored in the private database of your personal iCloud account, which is provided and operated by Apple.

We do not have access to your iCloud account or to any data RootBase stores in it. Data stored in iCloud is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and the iCloud terms you have agreed to with Apple.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not have access to any of your data. There are no advertising partners, analytics providers, or affiliated entities that receive information from RootBase. If you turn on iCloud Sync, your data is transmitted directly between your devices and your own iCloud account; it is not sent to us.

Camera, Photos, and Location

RootBase asks for access to your camera and photo library only so you can add photos of your plants. Photos you add are stored with your plant data as described above.

Photo Location is off by default. If you turn it on, RootBase reads your location when you take a photo in the app and saves it in that photo's file. The location stays with the photo, including when you share it or when it syncs through iCloud. RootBase does not track your location otherwise and never sends it to us.

Notifications

Care reminders are off until you turn them on. They are scheduled on your device, and no reminder content is sent to us.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since your data resides on your device and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud account, you have complete control over it at all times. You can delete individual plants and their associated care history within the app. Uninstalling RootBase removes all app data from your device. If you have used iCloud Sync, turning it off or uninstalling the app does not remove your data from iCloud; you can manage or delete it through your device's iCloud settings. There is no RootBase account to delete and no data on our servers to request removal of.

Children's Privacy

RootBase does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected by us or transmitted to us, the app does not pose privacy concerns for users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date. If we ever introduce features that change how data is handled, we will update this policy before those features are released.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at support@rootbaseapp.com.
